# Kajax SEO And Marketing Research 2026

Last updated: 2026-06-13

This document summarizes competitor patterns and current SEO/marketing direction for a small-but-capable woodworking business that wants to move from word-of-mouth custom jobs toward B2B wooden components, short runs and better qualified construction/custom leads.

## Executive Direction

Kajax should not try to look like a generic local carpenter. The strongest direction is:

1. Production-capable workshop for B2B wooden components and short runs.
2. Practical subcontractor for advertising/event/POS wood elements, not a full POS agency.
3. Local construction joinery specialist for Pomorskie: stairs, doors, trims and custom woodwork.
4. Custom/artistic joinery proof for architects and demanding projects.

The site should stay fast, simple and conversion-focused, but the content needs more proof: photos, process, capacities, materials, example projects, packaging, timelines and real constraints.

## What Competitors Do Well

### B2B Production Competitors

KONAR uses a strong B2B manufacturing position: serial furniture/component production, wood/MDF, CNC, RAL/NCS finishing and export to Europe.

Pattern to borrow:

- clear B2B positioning above the fold,
- production proof numbers,
- specific capabilities,
- export/logistics signal,
- direct quote CTA,
- multilingual pages.

Reference:

- `https://konar-meble.pl/pl/`

### POS / Advertising / Event Wood Elements

KONAR's POS page is very close to the direction we want: wooden/MDF promotional stands, POS displays, agencies/brands, technical optimization, CNC, premium finishing, FAQ and CTA.

Dispos and PosPoint also show the market language: POS materials, displays, retail zones, mixed materials, packing, transport, brand support and sustainable/eco materials.

Pattern to borrow:

- landing pages for POS/display/event use cases,
- process sections: design optimization, production, finishing, packing,
- FAQ for agencies,
- language around premium retail exposure and campaign deadlines,
- strong image proof of finished displays and repeatable batches.

Important limitation:

- Do not claim print, LED, plexi, full POS agency or industrial finishing unless it is true. Kajax can position as the wood subcontractor/co-producer for these projects.

References:

- `https://konar-meble.pl/pl/pos-display-manufacturer/`
- `https://pospoint.pl/`
- `https://dispos.pl/ecopos/`

### Serial Wood Components / CNC / Packaging

Stolarmax signals a useful direction: serial wooden components for construction, advertising and transport, CNC services and custom transport packaging.

Pattern to borrow:

- "production of wooden elements" as its own offer,
- packaging/logistics as a commercial feature,
- visible machine/capacity details,
- industry-use categories.

Important limitation:

- Certifications like ISPM 15 should only be mentioned if Kajax actually has them.

Reference:

- `https://stolarmax.pl/`

### Local Construction Joinery

Local Pomorskie stair/joinery competitors tend to use separate pages for stairs, doors, variants, FAQ, region and quote CTAs. Stolmark also uses visual/3D proof and detailed stair categories. Konkol emphasizes local region, full process, materials and individual pricing.

Pattern to borrow:

- local landing pages for high-intent services,
- project stages: material choice, measurement, production, mounting,
- visible phone/quote CTA,
- FAQ,
- real photos,
- regional signals: Gościcino, Wejherowo, Trójmiasto, Pomorskie.

References:

- `https://www.stolmark.pl/`
- `https://konkol-produkt.pl/`

## Current SEO/AI Search Direction

Google's current guidance does not require special AI-only markup. The important direction is still: technically crawlable pages, helpful content, visible text, internal links, structured data matching visible content, good page experience, high-quality images and up-to-date Business Profile information.

What this means for Kajax:

- Keep static/server-rendered pages fast and crawlable.
- Build useful pages that answer real buyer questions, not keyword-stuffed blog filler.
- Put important information in visible HTML, not only in images.
- Keep structured data aligned with visible content.
- Use real images with descriptive filenames and alt text.
- Treat Search Console + GA4 conversion quality as more important than raw traffic.

References:

- `https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide`
- `https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content`
- `https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features`

- `https://web.dev/articles/vitals`

## Conversion Pattern To Match Or Beat

Pages should use:

- one primary CTA above the fold,
- phone always visible,
- short form with optional fields,
- "what to send" checklist,
- FAQ close to objections,
- internal links to next logical pages,
- no public placeholder descriptions under images,
- real photos as soon as provided.

For B2B pages, every page should answer:

- What can you make?
- For whom?
- What input do you need?
- What affects price and deadline?
- Can it repeat?
- Can it be packed/shipped?
- What should not be requested?

## Content Style Rules

Use:

- concrete workshop/process language,
- real constraints,
- "we can assess this if you send X",
- "this may not be a good fit if Y",
- short lead sections,
- examples of components,
- buying-stage guides.

Avoid:

- generic luxury copy,
- empty claims like "najwyższa jakość",
- fake factory scale,
- overpromising Europe-wide logistics before it is confirmed,
- blog posts that only repeat keywords.
